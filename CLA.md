# Ordo Individual Contributor License Agreement

This is the **Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License
Agreement, version 2.0** — the standard, widely-recognized ICLA that thousands
of projects use — with the entity name and the contact details changed and
nothing else. It is reproduced below in full.

If you want the plain-English explanation of what signing this does and does
not mean, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first. This file is the
legal text; that one is the human one.

The Apache original, for comparison:
<https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf> (the ASF now publishes V2.2; the
V2.0 text below is the version this project adopts, and the operative
sections 1–8 are materially the same in both).

---

## What was changed from the Apache original

Every difference from the ASF text, listed so you can check it against the
original rather than take this file's word for it:

1. **"The Apache Software Foundation" / "the Foundation" → "Hominem".**
   Hominem is the copyright holder of record for Ordo (see `NOTICE`). Hominem is the trade name under which Ordo is published; a DBA registration is pending. The counterparty named here is that trade name and its owner. If an entity is formed later, this agreement's assignment clause is what lets the rights move to it — that is one of the reasons it exists.
2. **Contact and delivery details.** The ASF's secretary address, fax number
   and postal address are replaced with `secobs@hominem.ai`, and the ASF's
   "preferred Apache id" field is replaced with a GitHub username field.
3. **One clause narrowed, because it could not be true as written.** The
   Apache text says the Foundation "shall not use Your Contributions in a way
   that is contrary to the public benefit or inconsistent with its nonprofit
   status and bylaws." Hominem is not a nonprofit and has no bylaws, so that
   half of the sentence is dropped rather than falsely asserted. The public-
   benefit undertaking is kept.
4. **Section 4's reference to a Corporate CLA** is kept verbatim. Ordo does
   not yet publish a Corporate CLA; if your employer needs one, write to
   `secobs@hominem.ai` and one will be provided.

Nothing in sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 or 8 has been altered beyond the entity
name.

## How to sign

Email a completed copy of the form above to **secobs@hominem.ai**, from the
address you want on file, with the subject line `Ordo ICLA`. A typed name in
the signature line is accepted; a scanned wet signature is not required.

You sign once. It covers every contribution you make to Ordo, before and
after, so you will never be asked again.

If your employer owns the intellectual property you create — check your
contract, most do — see section 4. Write to `secobs@hominem.ai` and a
Corporate CLA will be provided.
