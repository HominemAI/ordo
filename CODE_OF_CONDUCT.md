# Code of Conduct

Be decent to people. Assume the person who disagreed with you read the same
document differently, not that they are arguing in bad faith. Criticize the
work — the design, the patch, the sentence — and never the person who wrote
it; "this breaks under concurrent writes" is always available, and the version
with an insult in it is never better. Harassment, personal attacks, and
deliberate bad-faith participation are not welcome here in any amount.
Maintainers enforce this, up to and including removing people, and they will
not litigate it in public.

That is the whole of the spirit. For the parts a paragraph cannot cover —
the detailed definitions of unacceptable behaviour, scope, and the
enforcement ladder — **the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc] applies in full,
and governs wherever this summary is silent.**

Two other communities say the same thing at more length and say it well; both
are worth reading and neither contradicts the above:

- [The Django Code of Conduct](https://www.djangoproject.com/conduct/)
- [The Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct)

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **secobs@hominem.ai**. Reports are read
by the maintainer. You do not need to have a formal case, a citation, or a
thick skin about it first — if something felt wrong, that is enough of a
reason to write.

Reports are handled confidentially. Retaliating against someone for making a
report in good faith is itself a violation.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
