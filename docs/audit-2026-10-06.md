# Audit 2026-10-06: the batch of five

The batch audited was five features landed 2026-10-06. They are pause answers per reason, a leaver keeping their name and a deleted account being kept, a bucket that holds files being changeable, a revert past a money rescale, and erasure leaving files in a tenant-owned bucket.
3 defects fixed, 2 decisions for the owner, the rest sound.

## Findings

### 1. A deleted account could get back in through an invitation ticket.

- **Severity:** High
- **Fix:** commit `f7c0aae8`
- **What happened:** `register_with_ticket` (crates/ordo-host/src/auth.rs) and `Directory::insert_link` (crates/ordo-identity/src/directory.rs) never asked the deleted mark, so a ticket naming a deleted principal registered a fresh passkey and minted a session. Now the directory refuses the link, and the host refuses before the ticket is spent, recorded as `identity-deleted`.
- **A regression would look like:** a ticket that names a deleted principal answers 200 with a token.
- **Guarded by:** `a_deleted_account_is_given_no_new_authenticator_link` (ordo-identity), `a_ticket_that_names_a_deleted_account_registers_nothing` (ordo-host, tests/auth_passkey.rs)

### 2. The registration sweep removed a live person who had left every tenant.

- **Severity:** High
- **Fix:** commit `125ac47f`
- **What happened:** the sweep (crates/ordo-host/src/registrations.rs) was ruled for sign-ups no tenant admits, kept 90 days (2026-09-29). A left seat holds `left:<principal>:<seat>`, which it read as no admission.
- So a leaver was removed 90 days after sign-up, possibly the day after leaving, with a record saying `registration-expired`. The 2026-10-06 ruling keeps an account until the person deletes it.
- `admitted_anywhere` now reads the person out of a left seat (`registrations::person_of_seat`).
- **A regression would look like:** a person with only `left` seats and an old sign-up disappears from `$principal` after a sweep pass.
- **Guarded by:** `a_person_who_was_admitted_and_then_left_every_tenant_is_kept_by_the_sweep` (tests/registrations.rs)
- **Residual:** a leaver whose only tenant was later erased has no seat left to read, and looks like a sign-up nobody admitted again. Low; see decision 5.

### 3. The deployment's record of a bucket change no longer said who made the call.

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fix:** commit `03887a8c`
- **What happened:** since 73fbc0dc the deployment's stream holds the only record of an attach, replace or detach, and `BucketOps::record_change` (crates/ordo-host/src/bucket_ops.rs) named the platform as subject. It now names the member, as the erasure and identity-removal records do.
- **A regression would look like:** a custody entry on the deployment stream with subject `system:attach-bucket`.
- **Guarded by:** `the_deployments_record_of_a_bucket_change_names_the_member_who_asked` (tests/attach_bucket.rs)

### 4. An export carries no `$places`, so its chain cannot be read at the right places after a rescale.

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fix:** **Decision needed.** **Add a `places.jsonl` member to the export bundle, holding the `$places` rows (entity, field, from_places, places, choice, money, heads), and have the reader apply them.**
- **Why:** the bundle's `commandlog.jsonl` holds images in the minor units of their day, and nothing in the bundle says where the places changed.
- **What happened:** `export_space` (crates/ordo-exec/src/export.rs) writes `commandlog.jsonl` and no `$places`. Import does not restore a chain, so a revert on the importing side is unaffected and a reader of the bundle is the one misled.
- **A regression would look like:** an exported chain of a money field rescaled 2 to 4 places reads 2999 and 299900 as the same kind of number.

### 5. The account removal setting is read once, when the account is deleted.

- **Severity:** Low
- **Fix:** **Decision needed.** **Compute the due time at sweep time from `deleted_at` and the current setting, and under `now` run one pass over `$accountremoval` at boot.**
- **Why:** `due_at` is fixed when the account is deleted, so shortening `<n>d` does not move it. A change from `<n>d` to `now` leaves the scheduled rows unrun, because the worker only starts under `<n>d`.
- **What happened:** `account_removal.rs` stores `due_at` on delete; `runtime.rs` starts `accounts.removal` only for `<n>d`. A change to `never` keeps everything, which matches the ruling.
- **A regression would look like:** an account deleted under `30d`, with the setting then changed to `now`, is still in `$principal` a day later.

## Sound

- Pause answers: the class stays `space_paused` whatever status is set; `paused_status` allows 4xx and 503 only; a 503 is never cached for replay. The reason word is a closed list (`PAUSE_NOTES`), so no free text reaches the answer.
- Pause answers and the clients: the SDK keys on error codes (only `403` with `reauthentication_required` starts a re-auth) and React has no 401 handling, so a 401 or 403 pause answer starts no re-auth loop.
- Sign-in of a deleted account: `sign_in` and `resolve_or_onboard`, step-up and recovery refuse it; its links stay, so its identifiers name nobody else. Sessions end through the deny list the door reads (`principal_ended`); share windows, links and factor routes need a session; with fix 1, a ticket is refused too.
- The removal worker: leader-only, 32 per pass with `more` set when full, idempotent because removal deletes the `$accountremoval` row last and a second pass finds nothing. No path clears `deleted_at`, so nothing not deleted is ever scheduled.
- The seat mark: every `left` to `deleted` move is one converge through `platform_write` and `Fixture::run_seat_deleted_on`; the write-protocol ratchet `only_the_write_protocol_updates_entity_rows` keeps its list unchanged.
- The leaver's name: kept by default, replaced only under `ORDO_MEMBER_LEFT_NAME`, bounded at 256 characters, refused at boot when longer; history keeps the before-image.
- The revert past a rescale: money only; `keep` converts nothing; a `$places` row without a chain position refuses (`places_revert_unplaced`); the chain heads are taken under the table lock; conversion in i128 with half-even rounding only when the app developer allows it, overflow refused.
- Erasure in a tenant-owned bucket: objects under `OwnPerimeter` are left, counted and digested per bucket; the record carries endpoint, bucket and prefix, count, epochs and a digest, no credential label and no key material; `PlatformSealed` objects are deleted as before.
- Bucket change with files held: `objects_held` is read to report, never to refuse; each file's `$blobref` row records its store; `verify --objects` follows that store and, without a credential provider, says so rather than answering from the deployment's own store.
- Tenant isolation: the bucket ops are owner-only at the door (`platform_ops`), the pause ops are gated by the roles the setting names, and each resolves its space from the door's bound space; `mark_seats` finds seats by the `left:<principal>:` prefix only.

Board, checklist and rulings were not touched by this audit; the two decisions above are for the owner to place on the board, and this file is owed its `board-docs` entry.
